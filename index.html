Dr. Nikita Hari

Re-engineering education. Engineering a better world.

I'm currently the Head of the Teaching, Research Design and Support Group in the University of Oxford's Department of Engineering Science. I work with universities worldwide to rethink engineering pedagogy, policy and practice around the needs of the people and places they serve. My vision is a generation of engineers with the imagination to ask what could be better and the judgement to build it together.
